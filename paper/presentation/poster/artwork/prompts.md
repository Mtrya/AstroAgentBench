# Artwork provenance

Both assets were generated using the built-in image generation tool, with the approved composition study as the visual reference. The artwork is illustrative; it does not encode measured or simulated geometry. Labels, orbits, observation cones, footprints, timing, and results are assembled separately as editable vectors.

## Earth background

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: reusable background art plate for the approved conference poster shown in the reference. Reference image is ONLY for visual style and scene lighting; output ONLY a clean Earth-and-space background plate, not a poster. Landscape approximately 4:3, at least 3000 pixels wide if possible. Match the beautiful realistic Earth limb and dark navy blue space from the reference poster's hero illustration. Oblique orbital view, Earth occupying lower 62% of image with curved luminous atmospheric limb sloping from upper-left/middle toward lower-right. Detailed natural green land, coastal fields, blue ocean, restrained white clouds. Upper 38% is clean dark navy space with very sparse tiny stars, enough open negative space for later satellite placement and white annotations. Lower terrain is visible but not cluttered. Soft cyan atmospheric glow; premium scientific editorial illustration with photoreal material detail, not a cartoon. IMPORTANT remove ALL satellites, dishes, equipment, foreground objects, orbit lines, beams, paths, cones, markers, grids, icons, timelines, annotations, labels, text, logos and poster panels. No UI. No planet floating as an entire tiny sphere. No painted arrows. Pure background environment only. The terrestrial geography is illustrative, not a claim of geospatial accuracy.

## Satellite sprite

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: reusable isolated spacecraft sprite for the approved poster reference. Reference is for spacecraft design, material quality, viewing angle and lighting, not a target poster to reproduce. Output exactly ONE Earth-observation satellite, centered, entire object visible with generous transparent padding, true alpha transparency around it. No background, no floor, no drop shadow on a surface, no painted checkerboard. Photoreal scientific editorial 3D illustration. Compact gold thermal-foil and silver spacecraft bus, small dark downward-looking optical instrument, two broad rectangular deep cobalt blue solar-panel wings extending diagonally left and right, readable realistic panel cell grid. Oblique three-quarter orbital view seen from slightly above and in front, spacecraft camera pointing down toward an implied Earth, solar wings almost horizontal with gentle tilt. Strong clean silhouette; illuminated upper-left, subtle cyan rim and natural gold highlights, matching the reference hero. No beam, no orbit path, no text, no labels, no thruster flames, no Earth. 1600 pixels square or greater if possible. Reusable sprite will be placed twice in a schematic observation scene.
